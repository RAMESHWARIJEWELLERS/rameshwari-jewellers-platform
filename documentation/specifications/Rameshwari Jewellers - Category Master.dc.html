Category identity

RuleDetail
Identity is the pathTwo terms with the same visible name under different parents are two different categories. Gold Jewellery › महिला के आभूषण › कलाई के आभूषण › कड़ा, the same path under Silver Jewellery, and the same under Black Polish Silver are three distinct terms.
Names are never alteredNo capitalization change, no spelling normalisation, no transliteration of a Hindi name, no merging. Where the master uses English group names (Ear Jewellery, Nose Jewellery, Neck Jewellery) they stay English; where it uses Hindi (सिर के आभूषण, कलाई के आभूषण) they stay Hindi.
Spelling variants preservedहथफूल under कलाई के आभूषण and हथफुल under अंगुली के आभूषण differ by one vowel and are kept exactly as supplied. बच्चो के आभूषण is spelled as the master spells it.
Slugs are path-derivedWordPress requires a unique slug per taxonomy. The importer generates one deterministically from the full ancestor path, so the visible name is untouched: gold-mahila-kalai-kada, silver-mahila-kalai-kada, bps-mahila-kalai-kada. Latin only — Devanagari slugs percent-encode badly on some shared hosts.
Import match keyThe full slug path. Re-running the import updates rather than duplicates, so the file can be loaded repeatedly as the taxonomy is refined.
DeduplicationDisabled. The importer never merges on name. Duplicate display names are expected and valid.
NestingUnbounded. This master reaches depth 5; nothing in the model special-cases a level. The depth-20 guard is a cycle detector, not a business limit.
Scale306 terms today, designed to 1,500, practical ceiling ~5,000 where the menu moves to on-demand level loading. Path and depth caches carry the load; counts are written hourly, never computed live.

Accepted consequence — the facet overlap

Recorded once, not re-argued. METALS duplicates the rj_metal taxonomy term for term, and FOR duplicates rj_audience; WEDDING partially duplicates rj_occasion and places Mangalsutra, Maang Tikka and Bridal Nath as roots-level children while मंगलसूत्र, टिका and नथ also exist deeper in the tree. Rule 13 requires the five real roots, so this stands as an accepted consequence of a locked decision.

What it means in practice: a product may need filing in two places, category counts under METALS and the metal facet can differ, and two pages can compete for the same query. Mitigation, to be specified before Stage 4: treat the METALS, WEDDING and FOR branches as navigational — products are assigned to the facet taxonomies, and those three root branches resolve to filtered archives rather than holding their own product assignments. That keeps one storage location per fact while leaving the five-root structure exactly as the master specifies. This needs your confirmation but does not block the import file, since the terms are created either way.
