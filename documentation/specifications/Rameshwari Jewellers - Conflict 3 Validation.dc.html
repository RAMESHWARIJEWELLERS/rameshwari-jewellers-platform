Conflict 3 — RESOLVED by owner decision

Scheduled hero campaigns are an approved business requirement. That answers the open question in §1 and reverses this document's recommendation on storage: rj_page_section is created as a post type, and the rj_sections options group is not created. All other rejections stand.

Sections 1–7 below are preserved as the reasoning record. Sections A–G carry the final decision and are authoritative where the two differ.

Two findings from the validation — one held, one was overtaken

1. OVERTAKEN by the owner decision. The validation argued that adding a tenth options group would use the approved architecture where a post type would extend it, and that revisions and scheduling were refinements I had introduced rather than approved requirements. The second half is now false: scheduled hero campaigns are an approved business requirement, and that justifies the permanent identifier. The argument is preserved because its first half still holds — a new post type is the heavier step and needed a requirement behind it. It now has one.

2. HELD. Notification audiences can come from _rj_notify_prefs — what the customer explicitly opted into — rather than from an admin-assigned segment inferred about them. That is both simpler and better on consent. _rj_segment is therefore not required, and is not created.

2 · rj_hero

A hero slide is an editable presentation band with an image, copy and a CTA — structurally identical to the other bands. Whichever container is chosen, hero belongs in it as three keyed records. A separate rj_hero identifier creates two representations of one concept.

NO NEW rj_hero. Hero campaigns are records inside rj_page_section carrying _rj_section_key = hero — a single allow-list value, with any number of dated campaigns under it. Not hero_1/hero_2/hero_3: fixed keys would cap campaigns at three, which the approved requirement forbids. Authoritative definition: Development Blueprint §6.2.

3 · rj_reel_type

The conclusion stands, but I should correct the premise in your brief rather than agree with it. _rj_source_type does not cover this. They are different axes:

FieldAxisValues
_rj_source_typeWhere the video comes from — a technical field the player reads to decide how to embedupload · instagram · youtube · url
The proposed rj_reel_typeWhat the video is about — an editorial field a visitor would filter byStyling · Craftsmanship · Bridal · Store Tour · Customer

A customer browsing reels does not care whether a video was uploaded or embedded from Instagram. So _rj_source_type cannot stand in for editorial categorisation.

The rejection holds for a different reason: rj_tag and rj_occasion are both already registered on reels, and either can carry these five values today. More importantly, filtered reel browsing appears in no approved requirement — it is a refinement I proposed, and by your rule 4 it does not clear the bar for a permanent identifier.

NO NEW rj_reel_type. Use rj_tag. Revisit if the reel library passes roughly 200 items and tag-based filtering proves inadequate.

4 · rj_location

Confirmed. rj_showroom already carries _rj_address, _rj_city, _rj_state, _rj_pincode, _rj_lat and _rj_lng in the approved meta registry. Local-business structured data is generated from those fields directly; a term adds nothing the markup reads.

A location taxonomy would also duplicate showroom data by definition — the term value would be a copy of the city and locality already stored on the post — and would generate archive URLs such as /location/jhotwara/ that list exactly one showroom and duplicate that showroom's own page. Thin duplicate content competing with the page you want ranking.

NO NEW rj_location. Reconsider at five or more branches across multiple cities.

5 · Customer segment

Is it an approved requirement?

Partly. The approved Notification Architecture states that a bridal collection announcement goes to a Bridal Interest audience. The audience is approved. Admin-assigned segmentation as the mechanism for producing it is not — that was my implementation choice.

OptionSemanticsAssessment
A · rj_customer_segment taxonomyAdmin-assigned classificationRejected. User taxonomies are not first-class in WordPress: no built-in admin UI, unreliable term counts for non-post objects, weaker REST behaviour. Would need a custom screen to replicate what meta gives free.
B · _rj_segment user metaAdmin-assigned classificationTechnically sound, but see below — the requirement it was meant to serve is already met.
C · _rj_notify_prefsCustomer-declared interestAlready approved, already in the meta registry §9.5, already the field the notification worker reads. A customer who wants bridal announcements ticks bridal. That is a better audience than one the shop inferred — more accurate, and consent-based rather than profiling-based.
D · Existing customer architecture—Wishlist, recent views and lead history already let the admin panel answer "who has shown bridal interest" as a filtered report, without storing a classification on the person.

Verdict

NO NEW rj_customer_segment, and no _rj_segment either. Option C meets the approved requirement using a field that already exists. Option D covers the admin's reporting need without storing anything new.

This also removes a privacy question that was not worth carrying: a stored label such as High Intent or Dormant is personal data the customer never agreed to and would see in a data-export request. Not storing it is simpler and cleaner.

Segments remain available later as a pure marketing feature if the shop starts running campaigns that opt-in preferences genuinely cannot target. At that point it is one meta key and a short backfill.

C · Identifiers NOT to create

rj_hero · rj_reel_type · rj_location · rj_customer_segment · _rj_segment · rj_sections options group

Hero is a section key on rj_page_section, not a second post type. The other four have a written trigger for revisiting, recorded above. Each is cheap to add later and expensive to remove once live — that asymmetry is the whole argument for leaving them out now. The risk to accept honestly: three capabilities now arrive as conventions rather than as schema, and conventions drift where schema does not. The triggers are the mitigation, and they only work if they are honoured.
