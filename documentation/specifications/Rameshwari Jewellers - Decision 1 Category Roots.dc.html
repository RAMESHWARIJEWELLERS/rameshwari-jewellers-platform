Conflict 4 — three roots duplicate approved facet taxonomies

Existing decision

Development Blueprint §7.1 registers rj_metal, rj_audience and rj_occasion as flat facet taxonomies on products. §8.2 separately lists METALS, WEDDING and FOR as roots of rj_category.

The conflict

Category rootIts children, per the briefApproved facet taxonomy
METALSGold · Diamond · Silver · Platinum · Gemstone · White Gold · Rose Goldrj_metal — the identical seven terms
FORBaby · Kids · Girls · Boys · Men · Women · Bride · Groomrj_audience — the identical eight terms
WEDDINGBridal Set · Mangalsutra · Couple Rings · Engagement Rings · Bridal Nath · Maang Tikka · Anniversaryrj_occasion partially (Bridal, Anniversary). The other five are product types that already exist deeper in the Hindi tree — मंगलसूत्र under Neck, टिका under Head, नथ under Nose

Why it matters

If both exist and both are assigned, every gold ring is filed twice — once at METALS → Gold and once as rj_metal: Gold. Two records of one fact means two counts that drift apart, two sets of filter results that disagree, and two pages competing for the same search query. It is exactly the redundant representation the locked rules forbid, and it would be discovered six months in, after 3,000 products had been filed.

WEDDING is worse than a duplicate: Mangalsutra as a category root child and मंगलसूत्र under Gold → Women's → Neck are the same product type in two places in the same taxonomy. A product must be filed in one, and whichever the staff member picks, the other page is empty.

Affected

Data\Taxonomies · Domain\Category · Services\MenuTree · the category import file · Development Blueprint §7.1, §8.2 and §8.5 · UI/UX §3 mega menu · the filter panel.

Migration impact

Now: none. No term exists yet. After products are filed: re-filing every product and rewriting every URL under three roots. This is the cheapest moment by a wide margin to settle it.
