Rameshwari Jewellers · Architecture Decision

Navigation / Facet Architecture

Twenty-two category terms overlap three facet taxonomies. One fact, one home — without touching the locked master.

1 · Problem

Three of the five locked roots describe the same facts as three approved facet taxonomies. If a product is assigned in both places, one fact has two homes: counts drift, filter results disagree, two pages compete for one query, and every product costs twice the filing effort. The overlap is 22 child terms out of 306.

LockedDetail

TaxonomiesSeven, per Development Blueprint §7.1. rj_category hierarchical; rj_metal, rj_purity, rj_occasion, rj_audience, rj_collection_tax, rj_tag flat.

3 · Model A — products assigned to the category nodes

METALS › Gold holds real product relationships. rj_metal also holds them, for filtering.

AspectAssessment
Works out of the boxYes — ordinary WordPress. Archives, counts, breadcrumbs and REST all work with no custom resolution.
Single source of truthViolated. A gold ring's metal is recorded twice.
DriftCertain. Staff will assign one and forget the other. Within months METALS › Gold and the Gold filter return different sets, and neither is knowably right.
Filing costEvery product needs up to three extra assignments beyond its real category and facets.
SEOTwo indexable pages with the same products. Canonical can paper over it, but the underlying duplication remains.
VerdictRejected. It is the option that works today and fails in a year, and the failure is expensive to unwind once 3,000 products are filed.

4 · Model B — navigation resolver branches

The 22 terms exist as real rj_category terms — they must, the master locks them — but hold no product relationships. Their archives resolve dynamically to a facet query.

AspectAssessment
Single source of truthHeld. Metal lives in rj_metal and nowhere else.
NavigationUnchanged. All 306 terms are real, so the menu, the tree manager, drag-reorder and reparenting behave identically.
Filing costZero extra. Staff assign a category and the facets, as they would anyway.
Cost of the modelArchive rendering, counts and the product-grid query need a resolution step for 22 terms. One code path, applied in the query builder and the count job.
Where the mapping livesThe open question — see below. It cannot be name-matching alone, because WEDDING does not map cleanly.

5 · Hybrid

A hybrid would let some of the 22 hold products directly while others resolve. The only term that tempted it was Couple Rings, which has no existing facet term or category leaf. Adding Couple to rj_occasion resolves it — a term in a flat facet is ordinary content, not an architecture change, and the facet term list was never locked.

With that, all 22 resolve. No hybrid is necessary and none is proposed. A model where two sibling terms behave differently for no visible reason is the kind of inconsistency that produces bugs three years later.

The one addition this requires

KeyObjectDefinition
_rj_resolverTerm meta on rj_categoryJSON, empty on 284 terms and set on 22. Two shapes only — Shape A {"tax":"rj_metal","slug":"gold"} for a facet query, or Shape B {"terms":[derived IDs],"paths":["jewellery/gold-jewellery/…","jewellery/silver-jewellery/…","jewellery/black-polish-silver/…"]} for a union of category leaves. Shape B carries portable full slug paths as the migration and import source; IDs are derived and re-resolved from those paths on every load, because IDs alone are not portable. Validated against a schema; write capability rj_manage_categories; exposed read-only in the tree REST response so a client knows the archive is dynamic. Full definition: Resolver Approval & Lock §4.

Approved and locked — see Resolver Approval & Lock. It was tested for avoidability and is not avoidable: the alternatives are a slug or name convention, which WEDDING's three mapping cases defeat, or a hardcoded map in plugin code, which puts business data in a file where a theme change or a plugin update can lose it. A stored, schema-validated field is the only option consistent with the locked rules.

8 · SEO behaviour

ConcernHandling
Duplicate contentOne indexable URL per concept. The category path is canonical; the facet archive is noindex and canonicals to it. This is the decision Model A could not make cleanly, because there the duplication is in the data, not just the URLs.
Thin pagesA resolver term with no matching products is excluded from the menu by the same empty-term rule as any other term, and returns 404 rather than an empty archive.
Structured dataBreadcrumb markup is generated from the cached ancestor path, identical for resolver and editorial terms.
Per-term SEO fieldsUnchanged. All 306 terms carry _rj_seo_title, _rj_seo_desc and _rj_seo_noindex.
SitemapResolver terms are included when they resolve to at least one product.

9 · Product assignment rules

Answer to question D: no. A product is never assigned to METALS › Gold, WEDDING › Bridal Set or FOR › Women.

LayerEnforcement
Product editorThe category picker hides any term with a resolver, and the three roots, so the assignment cannot be made by hand.
REST and importerAssignment to a resolver term is rejected with 409 rj_conflict naming the facet to use instead. Not silently dropped — a silent drop is how an importer loses data without anyone noticing.
Primary term_rj_primary_term must be an editorial term. A resolver term is rejected.
RepairThe weekly sweep reports any relationship found on a resolver term, which can only arrive from a direct database write.
HealthDaily: every resolver resolves to a live facet term or live category terms. A broken mapping is a visible warning, never a silently empty page.

13 · Import and export

AspectBehaviour
Category fileGains one optional resolver column, populated on 22 of 306 rows. Path matching is unchanged.
Product fileSimpler than Model A — no METALS, WEDDING or FOR columns. Each product carries its editorial categories plus metal, purity, occasion and audience. One fact, one column.

14 · Portability and scale

ConcernAssessment
HostingNothing host-specific. Resolution is a term query, and counts are computed in the existing hourly job — 22 extra queries per hour, negligible on free shared hosting.
MigrationResolver values are term meta, carried by any standard database move. Facet references are stored by slug, not by ID, so they survive a re-import that changes term IDs.
1,000+ categoriesThe resolver set stays at 22 regardless of how the JEWELLERY tree grows. It does not scale with the catalogue.
CachingResolver archives cache like any archive, keyed on the term. Facet term changes bump the same version.
Data portabilityBetter than Model A: an export contains each fact once, so a future system does not have to work out which of two conflicting assignments is correct.

Four distinct concepts ANALYSIS QUESTION F

ConceptStored asWhat it answers

Conflating the first two is what produced this problem: the business's navigation groups its catalogue by four different axes, but only one of those axes is a filing decision.

15 · Final decision

DECISION: MODEL B

The 22 overlapping terms exist as real rj_category terms — the locked master requires it — but hold no product relationships. Their archives resolve to the corresponding facet query. Each fact keeps one authoritative home, the five-root navigation is exactly as locked, and the 306-term count is unchanged.

Model A is rejected for storing metal, audience and occasion twice. A hybrid is unnecessary once Couple is added to rj_occasion.

The one honest cost: a builder-native Gutenberg query loop pointed at a resolver term returns empty rather than erroring, because core resolves by term relationship. Our own block, widget and shortcode all handle it; the Health screen records the limitation.

16 · Exact implementation rule

LOCKED RULE
  1. All 306 Category Master terms are registered in rj_category. The master is unchanged.
  2. The 22 children of METALS, WEDDING and FOR carry _rj_resolver. Every other term has it empty. Shape A stores tax + slug; Shape B stores terms + portable paths, with IDs re-resolved from paths on load.
  3. No product is ever assigned to a resolver term or to one of the three roots. Blocked in the editor, rejected with 409 over REST and in the importer, rejected as a primary term, reported by the weekly sweep.
  4. A resolver archive queries the facet named in its resolver. Resolution happens in Domain\Category and Frontend\Query only — never duplicated into a widget, block, shortcode or template.
  5. Counts for resolver terms are computed in the existing hourly job from the resolved query, not from term relationships.
  6. Facet archives are noindex and canonical to the equivalent category path. The category URL is the one indexable page.
  7. Breadcrumbs for a product always follow _rj_primary_term, which is always editorial.
  8. Couple is added to rj_occasion. A term in a flat facet is content, not schema.
  9. Health verifies daily that all 22 resolvers resolve. A broken mapping is a visible warning, never an empty page.

Summary

DECISION: MODEL B

Approved and locked. _rj_resolver, one term meta key on rj_category, set on 22 of 306 terms. See Resolver Approval & Lock for the full schema and the twelve implementation clauses. Nothing else in the locked architecture changes: 5 roots, 306 terms, 7 taxonomies, 6 post types, 8 tables.
