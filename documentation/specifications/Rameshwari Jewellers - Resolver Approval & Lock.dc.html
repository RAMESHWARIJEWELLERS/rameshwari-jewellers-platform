2 · Why it is required

Model B requires that 22 navigation terms resolve their archives from the facet taxonomies rather than from their own product relationships. Something has to record which facet each of the 22 resolves to. Three ways to record it were tested:

MechanismOutcome
Slug or name conventionFails. METALS and FOR map one-to-one by name, but WEDDING has three different mapping cases in one branch — three children map to an occasion term, three to a union of category leaves, one to a facet term that does not yet exist. A convention cannot express three shapes, and it breaks silently on a rename.
Hardcoded array in plugin codeFails. Puts business data in a code file: lost or overwritten on update, absent from exports, not editable by the owner, and in direct breach of the theme-independence and portability rules. Explicitly forbidden in your brief.
Stored term metaWorks. 22 rows of schema-validated JSON on terms that already exist. Travels with a standard database move, appears in exports, editable through the tree manager, readable over REST.

It is the only addition, and it is unavoidable given the constraints. Without it Model B cannot be built, and Model A is already rejected for storing each fact twice.

FieldTypeValidation
taxstringAllow-list: rj_metal, rj_purity, rj_occasion, rj_audience. rj_category, rj_collection_tax and rj_tag are rejected — the first would allow circular resolution, and the other two are not navigation facets.
slugstringMust name a live term in that taxonomy. Slug, never ID — so the mapping survives a re-import that changes term IDs.

Shape B — category union resolver

{ "terms": [ 412, 587, 703 ] }
FieldTypeValidation
termsinteger array2–12 entries. Each must be a live rj_category term, must be a leaf (no children), must sit inside JEWELLERY or OTHERS, must not itself carry a resolver, and must not be the term being edited or any of its ancestors or descendants.
Clarification — Shape B stores IDs, and that is a deliberate exception

Check 27 asks that mappings survive term-ID changes by using stable slugs. Shape A does. Shape B cannot: its targets are three different leaves with the same visible name and the same slug fragment — मंगलसूत्र under Gold, Silver and Black Polish Silver. A bare slug does not identify which. Resolution: Shape B stores IDs and a parallel paths array of full slug paths, written at the same time. The importer resolves paths to IDs on every load, so IDs are derived and re-established after any migration while paths remain the portable record. Health compares the two daily and reports a mismatch. This satisfies check 27 in substance; storing only slugs would be ambiguous, and storing only IDs would be unportable.

No third shape exists. Anything else is rejected at write.

7 · Product assignment prohibition

No product is ever assigned to a resolver term or to METALS, WEDDING or FOR. Enforced at five independent layers, because a rule enforced in one place is a rule that leaks through the other four.

LayerEnforcement
Product editorThe category tree picker hides every term carrying a resolver and the three roots. The assignment cannot be made by hand.
Domain repositoryDomain\Product\Repository::save() strips and rejects resolver terms before any write. This is the layer everything else passes through, so it is the real guarantee.
REST409 rj_conflict, naming the term and the facet to use instead. Not silently dropped.
ImporterFails in the dry run with the row number and the correct facet column. Nothing is written, so a bad file costs minutes rather than a day.
Primary term_rj_primary_term must be an editorial term. A resolver term is rejected — which is what keeps breadcrumbs and canonicals correct.
Weekly sweepReports any relationship found on a resolver term. It can only arrive from a direct database write, and it will be visible within seven days.

17 · Final locked rule

DECISION: _rj_resolver APPROVED

LOCKED RULE

_rj_resolver is the single stored mapping mechanism for the 22 navigation resolver terms. It is term meta on rj_category, is not product data, does not create product relationships, and must be resolved centrally by the domain/query layer.

Implementation clauses
  1. All 306 Category Master terms are registered in rj_category. The master is unchanged.
  2. The 22 children of METALS, WEDDING and FOR carry _rj_resolver. The three roots and all 284 other terms have it empty.
  3. Two shapes only. Shape A stores tax and slug; Shape B stores terms plus portable paths. Schema-validated on every write.
  4. Shape A may target only rj_metal, rj_purity, rj_occasion or rj_audience. Never rj_category — which is what makes circular resolution structurally impossible.
  5. No product is ever assigned to a resolver term or to one of the three roots. Enforced in the editor, the domain repository, REST (409), the importer dry run, and the primary-term validator.
  6. Resolution occurs in Domain\Category and Frontend\Query only — never in a widget, block, shortcode or template.
  7. Counts use the same resolver logic, in the existing hourly job.
  8. Resolver archives keep the locked /c/… path. Facet archives are noindex and canonical to them.
  9. Product breadcrumbs always follow _rj_primary_term, which is always editorial.
  10. Couple is added to rj_occasion. A term in a flat facet is content, not schema.
  11. Export and import preserve _rj_resolver, Shape B as paths.
  12. Health verifies all 22 resolvers daily. A broken mapping warns and 404s; it never renders an empty archive.
